## In short

Most Walmart problems are returns: start them in the app, at walmart.com/returns or in a store, inside the item's window (90 days for most items, shorter for electronics, phones, appliances and most Marketplace items). Bring the receipt or order number; refunds go back to the original payment method. If that fails, call 1-800-WALMART, then dispute the charge with your card issuer. Walmart does not send recall or prize texts and never asks you to pay bills with Walmart Gift Cards.

## How complaints actually go

- Walmart's contact page lists customer service at 1-800-WALMART (1-800-925-6278), with ASL video support, and the walmart.com Help Center for answers and online chat. [Walmart: contact](https://corporate.walmart.com/about/contact)
- Returns start in the Walmart app, at walmart.com/returns or in a store; major appliances must be returned through the app or online, not in a store. Without a walmart.com account there is a separate return form. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)
- Marketplace (third-party seller) items follow the seller's return policy shown on the order, and Marketplace sellers may charge restocking fees of up to 20%. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)
- Refund timing after a receipted return: cash immediately, credit card up to 7 days, Walmart Gift Card or Walmart Balance up to 3 hours. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)

## What tends to work

- Without a receipt, a store can look up card purchases, and online orders can be found by phone number or email; the receipt lookup tool also finds store receipts. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)
- Ask for the in-store price match when an identical in-stock item is cheaper on Walmart.com; tell the associate at checkout. Walmart does not match competitors or Marketplace sellers. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)
- An unused Walmart+ membership can be refunded within 90 days if it was not used or activated; cancel and ask in the same contact. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)

## Traps and fine print

- Shorter return windows: major appliances 2 days after accepted delivery, most wireless phones 14 days, and consumer electronics, PCs, vacuums, most Marketplace items and Walmart Protection Plans 30 days. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)
- Non-receipted returns need a government photo ID and are refunded on a Walmart gift card, not in cash. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)
- The holiday extension (items bought October 1 through December 31 returnable until January 31) excludes wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, Dyson products and some seasonal and luxury goods. [Walmart policies](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies)

## By the numbers

- ACSI 2026 (interviews through December 2025): Walmart stores scored 76 out of 100 (up from 73), and Walmart online scored 77 (up from 75). The general merchandise retail average was 79. [ACSI Retail and Consumer Shipping Study 2026](https://theacsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/26jan_Retail-Shipping-Study-Final.pdf)
- BBB Scam Tracker: Walmart was the No. 8 most impersonated organization or brand in 2025, with 310 reports. [BBB Scam Tracker Risk Report 2025](https://bbbmarketplacetrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Risk-Report-2025-US.pdf)
- On June 20, 2025 Walmart agreed to pay $10 million to settle FTC charges that scammers used its in-store money transfer services (as an agent of MoneyGram, Western Union and Ria) to take money from consumers; the order bars Walmart from sending or paying out transfers it knows, or consciously avoids knowing, are fraud-induced. [FTC press release, June 20, 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/06/walmart-pay-10-million-settle-ftc-allegations-it-allowed-scammers-obtain-millions-consumers-using)

## Scams that target customers

- Walmart says it does not notify contest winners by text and does not send text messages about product recalls; such texts are smishing. [Walmart fraud alerts](https://corporate.walmart.com/privacy-security/fraud-alerts)
- Walmart says it will never mail you a check and ask you to deposit it to buy an item or service, and that you will never be asked to pay utility bills, bail or other bills with Walmart Gift Cards. [Walmart fraud alerts](https://corporate.walmart.com/privacy-security/fraud-alerts)
- Send fraudulent Walmart emails as an attachment to OnlineAbuse@walmart.com, and report Walmart Gift Card scams at (888) 537-5503. [Walmart fraud alerts](https://corporate.walmart.com/privacy-security/fraud-alerts)
