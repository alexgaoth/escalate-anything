## In short

Call 800-531-5000 or use chat; the same number handles cancellations, and cost-related cancellations are routed to a "personalized cancellation" call. DIRECTV does not prorate refunds, so cancel near the end of the billing cycle. Return leased equipment fast: satellite receivers within 21 days of termination, DIRECTV STREAM devices within 14 days. DIRECTV representatives never ask you to pay upfront for a discount and do not accept gift cards.

## How complaints actually go

- Customer service is 800-531-5000 (also the cancellation line); chat is on the customer service page; the main number is 1-800-DIRECTV (800-347-3288). [DIRECTV customer service](https://www.directv.com/customer-service) [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/)
- DIRECTV says most bill or service concerns can be resolved by chat or 800-531-5000; otherwise the dispute process starts with a Notice of Dispute and at least 60 days for DIRECTV to investigate, then a Demand for Arbitration to DIRECTV and the AAA. [DIRECTV dispute information](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/legal/dtv-arbitration-information_resolving-dispute.pdf)
- The Notice of Dispute goes to DIRECTV, LLC c/o CT Corporation, 1209 Orange Street, Wilmington, DE 19801, using the form or a signed letter with your account number, claim and requested relief. [DIRECTV dispute information](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/legal/dtv-arbitration-information_resolving-dispute.pdf)
- Arbitration terms in agreements before November 7, 2023 are posted separately; the current Residential Terms of Service govern newer accounts. [DIRECTV legal policy center](https://www.directv.com/legal/legal-policy-center/)

## What tends to work

- DIRECTV's cancel page routes cost-related cancellations to 800.531.5000 for a personalized cancellation; ask what offers are available on that call. [DIRECTV cancel help](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help)
- Cancelling during a free trial ends service immediately with no charge. [DIRECTV cancel help](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help)
- DIRECTV sends return instructions and prepays shipping by UPS, FedEx or US Mail; keep the tracking number. [DIRECTV cancel help](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help)
- If someone opened a DIRECTV account in your name, file an identity theft claim at accountverification.directv.com. [DIRECTV: recognize and avoid scams](https://www.directv.com/support/article/000086841)

## Traps and fine print

- DIRECTV gives no prorated credit or refund for partial periods unless the law requires it; service stays on until the end of the billing cycle. [DIRECTV cancel help](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help)
- Customers with a contract may owe a fee for the months left on it. [DIRECTV cancel help](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help)
- The equipment lease agreement (2016 PDF) charges $45 per receiver and $135 per DVR not received within 21 days of termination or returned damaged. [DIRECTV equipment lease agreement](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/dtv/directv_marketing/agreements_and_policies/EquipLeaseAgrmt_English.pdf)
- DIRECTV STREAM devices must be returned within 14 days after cancellation; otherwise the non-return fee is $120. [DIRECTV STREAM cancellation policy](https://www.directv.com/legal/directv-stream-cancellation-policy/)
- In arbitration the consumer is generally responsible for the $200 AAA filing fee. [DIRECTV dispute information](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/legal/dtv-arbitration-information_resolving-dispute.pdf)

## Scams that target customers

- DIRECTV representatives will never reach out to ask for personal or account information, ask you to pay upfront for discounted services, promotions or replacement equipment, or accept prepaid or retail gift cards as payment. [DIRECTV: recognize and avoid scams](https://www.directv.com/support/article/000086841)
- Scammers ask for advance payment for "discounted services or upgraded equipment", often by gift card, and may give a toll-free number to seem real. Contact DIRECTV only through the number on your bill or the official website. [DIRECTV: recognize and avoid scams](https://www.directv.com/support/article/000086841)
- DIRECTV says to report phishing or social engineering at IC3.gov and email fraud to reportfraud.ftc.gov and reportphishing@apwg.org. [DIRECTV: recognize and avoid scams](https://www.directv.com/support/article/000086841)
