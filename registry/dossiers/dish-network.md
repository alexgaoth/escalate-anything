## In short

Call 800-333-DISH; for a written escalation, email executivecustomerservice@dish.com. You can cancel by phone, by email to onlinecancellation@dish.com or by mail, but cancellation counts only from the day DISH receives it, and DISH gives no credit for the rest of the month. Return equipment within 30 days and keep tracking, because you carry the shipping risk. DISH bills monthly, so a call asking you to prepay months of service or pay by gift card is a scam.

## How complaints actually go

- Customer service is 800-333-DISH (800-333-3474); the Spanish line is 888-599-DISH; the agreement lists executivecustomerservice@dish.com as a customer service email. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- Written customer service mail goes to DISH Network L.L.C., Customer Service Center, P.O. Box 9033, Littleton, CO 80160-9033 (not the payment address). [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- A Dispute Resolution Notice must be a declaration signed under penalty of perjury, with your account number, a description of each disputed transaction, an itemized damages calculation and the resolution you want. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- The agreement bars arbitration or small claims for at least 60 days after the notice (the DISH notice form says 30 days), and you must personally take part in an individual settlement conference. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf) [DISH notice form](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/tv-plan-agreements/dispute-resolution-notice--English.pdf)

## What tends to work

- Cancel by email to onlinecancellation@dish.com so you have a dated written record; cancellation is effective no sooner than the date DISH receives the notice. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- DISH does not charge the early termination fee if service is disconnected within 24 hours after you accept the agreement. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- To check whether a call or offer is real, hang up and call 800-333-DISH yourself. [DISH consumer protection](https://support.dish.com/helpful-tools/consumer-protection)

## Traps and fine print

- DISH gives no credits, refunds or price reductions on cancellation, including for unused promotional discounts. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- An early termination fee may apply before a term commitment ends; the amount is in the promotion agreement. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- Leased equipment must be returned in good working order within 30 days; it is not returned until DISH receives it, you bear the risk of loss in shipment, and DISH charges up to $20.00 for each return label or empty box it sends. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- The agreement says DISH is not liable for any interruption in service and contains no outage credit. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- The arbitration opt-out window is 30 days after account setup or service start, whichever is first; a later postmark is invalid. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- The late payment fee is $12.00 ($10.00 in Virginia, Maine or Arizona). [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)

## Scams that target customers

- DISH will never call you asking for your account number, security code, receiver number or location ID, and will never require one particular payment method. [DISH consumer protection](https://support.dish.com/helpful-tools/consumer-protection)
- Scammers ask for upfront payment by money order or gift card, or offer deals that require paying 6 months or a year in advance; DISH bills monthly. [DISH consumer protection](https://support.dish.com/helpful-tools/consumer-protection)
- Criminals spoof DISH's phone number so a call looks like it comes from DISH, and send spoofed emails saying you must act now to keep an installation. DISH's page names dish@dishemail.com as a legitimate DISH sender. [DISH consumer protection](https://support.dish.com/helpful-tools/consumer-protection)
