## In short

Most bill shocks are a promotion that ended; check the promotion end date on the bill, then ask for current offers or switch. Dispute wrong charges in writing within the provider's deadline (as short as 30 days at Optimum), and request outage credits yourself, because several providers credit only on request. When you cancel, return every piece of equipment by the deadline and keep the receipt; unreturned-equipment fees run to hundreds of dollars. Real providers do not take gift cards and do not charge a fee to unlock a discount.

## How complaints work in this industry

- The FCC reviews each complaint and in many cases serves it on the provider, which has up to 30 days to respond to you and the FCC. Not every complaint is served. [FCC Consumer Help Center](https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov/hc/en-us/articles/202752940)
- The FCC asks you to try the provider first; filing an informal complaint is free. [FCC: filing an informal complaint](https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/filing-informal-complaint)
- State utility commissions often cannot help with internet or satellite TV. New York's Department of Public Service takes cable TV complaints but sends satellite TV and internet complaints to the NYS Attorney General. [NY DPS](https://dps.ny.gov/file-complaint)
- North Carolina's Public Staff sends broadband, internet, bundled package, cable and satellite TV complaints to the NC Attorney General's Office. [NC Public Staff](https://publicstaff.nc.gov/submit-complaint)
- Missouri's PSC does not regulate internet providers or cable television rates or service; Arizona's Corporation Commission consumer services cannot handle cable TV, satellite TV or internet complaints. [Missouri PSC](https://psc.mo.gov/General/Submit_A_Complaint) [Arizona Corporation Commission](https://azcc.gov/utilities/consumer-services)
- Some states do take cable TV complaints: the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities lists a toll-free cable TV complaint line, (800) 624-0331. [NJ BPU](https://www.nj.gov/bpu/assistance/complaints/)
- Billing dispute deadlines vary a lot: Optimum requires a written dispute within 30 days of receiving the bill; Spectrum within 60 days after the bill due date; Xfinity within 120 days of the bill date. [Optimum terms](https://www.optimum.com/terms-of-service/residential) [Spectrum annual notice](https://www.spectrum.com/policies/annual-notice) [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement)
- Before arbitration, every major provider requires a written notice and a waiting period: 60 days at Xfinity, Optimum and DIRECTV; 30 days after Spectrum receives a "Notice of Intent to Arbitrate". [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement) [Optimum terms](https://www.optimum.com/terms-of-service/residential) [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/legal/dtv-arbitration-information_resolving-dispute.pdf) [Spectrum terms](https://www.spectrum.com/policies/residential-general-terms-and-conditions-of-service)
- DISH's Dispute Resolution Notice must be a declaration signed under penalty of perjury, with an itemized calculation of damages. [DISH Residential Customer Agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)

## Your leverage

- Cable and satellite TV bills must show when a promotional price ends and must show the post-promotion rate 60 and 30 days before the introductory period ends (FCC "all-in" pricing rule, compliance required since December 19, 2024 for most providers). [47 CFR 76.310](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-76/subpart-H/section-76.310)
- Every broadband plan must have an FCC broadband label showing the monthly price, introductory rates, fees and typical speeds, at the point of sale and in your online account. [47 CFR 8.1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-8/section-8.1)
- Outage credits: Spectrum's Customer Commitment promises a credit for the full day when a neighborhood outage lasts more than two hours (excluding power outages, natural disasters and overnight scheduled maintenance). [Spectrum Customer Commitment](https://www.spectrum.com/our-customer-commitment)
- Xfinity gives a pro rata credit for an interruption of more than 24 consecutive hours if you request it within 120 days; its online outage tool applies some credits automatically. Its agreement lists separate outage-credit rules for Maine, New Jersey, New York and Ohio. [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement) [Xfinity outage credit](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/check-service-outage)
- A price change can let you leave: Xfinity says you may cancel if a material change hurts you, but continued use for more than 30 days after the change counts as acceptance. [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement)
- Some providers sell multi-year price guarantees to new customers, for example Xfinity's five-year guarantee launched April 15, 2025. Ask whether one applies before you agree to a new plan. [Comcast press release](https://corporate.comcast.com/press/releases/comcast-launches-five-year-guarantee-for-xfinity-internet-customers)
- Retention offers are real but hard to reach. On the Xfinity Community Forum, Xfinity staff say there is no direct retention number and route customers to a direct message to "Xfinity Support" to check for a new promotion. [Xfinity Community Forum](https://forums.xfinity.com/conversations/billing/retention-department-contact-information-needed/681a62e8ce99874d94d53b2b)
- Spectrum Community moderators route cancellations to the Customer Solutions team at 1-855-707-7328; DIRECTV's cancel page routes cost-related cancellations to 800.531.5000. [Spectrum Community](https://community.spectrum.net/discussion/178853/disconnect-service) [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help)
- Speed claims are enforceable. Under a stipulated order the FTC announced in May 2022, Frontier must substantiate internet speed claims customer by customer and let DSL customers who get speeds well below the advertised rate change or cancel at no charge. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/05/ftc-takes-action-against-frontier-lying-about-internet-speeds-ripping-customers-who-paid-high-speed)

## Traps

- At the end of a promotion, standard pricing applies automatically. [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement)
- Equipment return deadlines are short and fees are high: Xfinity's agreement requires return within 10 days after termination; Frontier charges up to $500 per device not returned within 30 days; DISH requires return within 30 days. [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement) [Frontier](https://frontier.com/helpcenter/account/how-to-return-frontier-equipment) [DISH agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- DIRECTV's equipment lease agreement charges $45 per receiver and $135 per DVR not received within 21 days; Optimum lists unreturned-equipment fees such as $345.00 for an HD/DVR receiver. [DIRECTV lease agreement](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/dtv/directv_marketing/agreements_and_policies/EquipLeaseAgrmt_English.pdf) [Optimum fees](https://www.optimum.com/pricing-packages)
- Equipment is not "returned" until the provider receives it. DISH says the customer bears the risk of loss in shipment, so keep the tracking number. [DISH agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- No refund for the rest of the month: DIRECTV gives no prorated credit for partial periods; Optimum says payments are nonrefundable for partially used periods; DISH gives no credits on cancellation, including for unused promotional discounts. [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help) [Optimum terms](https://www.optimum.com/terms-of-service/residential) [DISH agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf)
- Early termination fees can apply when you cancel before a promotional or contract term ends at Optimum, DISH, DIRECTV and Frontier. [Optimum terms](https://www.optimum.com/terms-of-service/residential) [DISH agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf) [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/customer/cancel-help) [Frontier](https://frontier.com/helpcenter/account/disconnecting-your-service)
- Some agreements limit outage remedies: Optimum's credits are at its sole discretion; DISH says it is not liable for any interruption in service; Frontier's internet terms reserve the right to refuse credits for interruptions. [Optimum terms](https://www.optimum.com/terms-of-service/residential) [DISH agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf) [Frontier internet terms](https://frontier.com/documents/corporate/terms/residential-internet-service-may-2025.pdf)
- Arbitration opt-out windows close 30 days after sign-up at Xfinity, Optimum, DISH and Spectrum. [Xfinity agreement](https://www.xfinity.com/corporate/customers/policies/subscriberagreement) [Optimum terms](https://www.optimum.com/terms-of-service/residential) [DISH agreement](https://www.dish.com/content/dam/dish/pdfs/service-agreements/2026/sep-10/6c%20-%20RCA%20-%20English%209.10.26.pdf) [Spectrum terms](https://www.spectrum.com/policies/residential-general-terms-and-conditions-of-service)
- DIRECTV arbitration: the consumer is generally responsible for the $200 AAA filing fee. [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/dtvassets/legal/dtv-arbitration-information_resolving-dispute.pdf)

## Scams to watch for

- On September 10, 2025 the FCC warned of robocalls, voicemails and texts offering "50% off" Comcast, Spectrum or Prime bills; callers then say the only way to keep the discount is to prepay several months with gift cards. The FCC calls pressure to act quickly and gift-card-only payment sure signs of a scam. [FCC alert](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-414472A1.pdf)
- "Discount if you pay now" calls. Spectrum says it will never ask you to pay a fee to get a discount; DIRECTV says its representatives will never ask you to pay upfront for discounted services or promotions. [Spectrum](https://www.spectrum.net/support/general/scam-and-fraud-alerts) [DIRECTV](https://www.directv.com/support/article/000086841)
- DISH warns about offers that require payment 6 months or a year in advance, or payment by money order or gift card; DISH bills monthly. [DISH](https://support.dish.com/helpful-tools/consumer-protection)
- Gift cards and payment apps are a red flag. Xfinity says it will not ask you to pay using merchant gift cards, wire transfer, PayPal, Venmo or Zelle; Spectrum does not accept cryptocurrency, gift cards, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App or PayPal; Optimum will never ask for payment by gift card, wire transfer or cryptocurrency. [Xfinity](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/protecting-from-fraud) [Spectrum](https://www.spectrum.net/support/general/scam-and-fraud-alerts) [Optimum](https://www.optimum.net/pages/phishing-alerts.html)
- Fake support numbers and payment sites in search results. Optimum tells customers not to use search engines to pay or to find contact numbers; use the number on the bill or the official contact page. [Optimum](https://www.optimum.net/pages/phishing-alerts.html)
- Spoofed caller ID: DISH says criminals can make a call appear to come from DISH's number; Xfinity says bad actors use numbers that look like Xfinity's to get account or payment information or place orders. [DISH](https://support.dish.com/helpful-tools/consumer-protection) [Xfinity](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/protecting-from-fraud)
- Equipment and package scams: Spectrum says it will never ask you to ship equipment back unless you contacted it first, and warns of "reshipping" fraud. [Spectrum](https://www.spectrum.net/support/general/scam-and-fraud-alerts)
- "Your equipment needs a paid fix" and tech-support calls: Optimum says it will never call to say there is an equipment issue that requires a payment. [Optimum](https://www.optimum.net/pages/phishing-alerts.html)
- Report phishing emails that imitate Spectrum to phishing@charter.net with full headers; report Xfinity-related phishing and scams through Xfinity's network abuse page. [Spectrum](https://www.spectrum.net/support/internet/email-security) [Xfinity](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/protecting-from-fraud)

## By the numbers

- ACSI 2026 (surveys April 2025 to March 2026): internet service providers scored 73 out of 100; fiber providers 76 and non-fiber providers 71. [ACSI study](https://theacsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26may_Telecom-Cell-Smartwatch-Study.pdf)
- ACSI 2026 fiber: AT&T Fiber 79, Xfinity Fiber 77, Google Fiber and Verizon Fios 75, Optimum Fiber 74, CenturyLink/Quantum Fiber and Frontier Fiber 72, Kinetic Fiber 71. [ACSI study](https://theacsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26may_Telecom-Cell-Smartwatch-Study.pdf)
- ACSI 2026 non-fiber: Verizon 5G Home Internet 79, T-Mobile 5G Home Internet 78, Starlink 76, Cox 72, AT&T Internet, Spectrum and Xfinity 71, CenturyLink 67, Optimum and Mediacom (Xtream) 64, Frontier 63. [ACSI study](https://theacsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26may_Telecom-Cell-Smartwatch-Study.pdf)
- ACSI 2026: 20% of non-fiber customers and 24% of fiber customers reported a complaint; complaint handling scored 58 (non-fiber) and 70 (fiber). [ACSI study](https://theacsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26may_Telecom-Cell-Smartwatch-Study.pdf)
- ACSI Entertainment Study 2026 (data July 2025 to June 2026): subscription TV scored 72; Verizon Fios 79, Frontier 74, Xfinity 72, DIRECTV satellite 71, DISH and Spectrum 69, Optimum 64. [ACSI press release](https://theacsi.com/news-and-resources/press-releases/2026/08/18/press-release-entertainment-study-2026/)
