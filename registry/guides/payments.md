## In short

Payment apps and money transfer services move money fast and rarely pull it back. An unauthorized transfer (someone else got into your account) has federal error-resolution rights much like a debit card; a payment you sent yourself after being tricked usually does not, and companies often deny scam losses under their terms. Report within minutes, ask the company to stop, cancel, recall or reverse the payment, and for an international transfer remember the 30-minute cancellation window. For goods, use the app's purchase-protected payment type or a credit card. If the company does not help, complain to the CFPB.

## How complaints work in this industry

- The CFPB sends each complaint to the company; companies generally respond in 15 days, and in some cases give a final response within 60 days. [CFPB: complaint process](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/process/)
- In 2025 the CFPB found that money transfer and payment app companies, responding to scam complaints, often denied liability by citing their terms of service and user negligence, suggested that consumers contact law enforcement or their bank, or said they could not reverse the transfers. [CFPB 2025 Consumer Response Annual Report](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_2025-cr-annual-report_2026-03.pdf)
- Zelle payments are handled by your own bank or credit union: report the transaction directly to it. [Zelle: report a fraud or scam](https://www.zelle.com/support/report-scam)
- If you paid a scammer through a payment app, report it to the app immediately and ask it to reverse the payment; for a wire transfer through a company such as Western Union or MoneyGram, report it to that company, say a scammer tricked you, and ask for a refund. [FTC: what to do if you were scammed](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed)
- If you think you sent money to a scammer, contact the bank or payment company right away and say there may have been an unauthorized transaction, then report to the FBI's IC3. [CFPB: common types of scams](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-are-some-common-types-of-scams-en-2092/)

## Your leverage

- CFPB guidance says any P2P payment that meets the definition of an electronic fund transfer is covered by Regulation E, and that a transfer made by a third party who got your account access through fraud (for example a stolen or tricked-out login) is unauthorized. [CFPB Electronic Fund Transfers FAQs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/deposit-accounts-resources/electronic-fund-transfers/electronic-fund-transfers-faqs/)
- For unauthorized transfers, report errors within 60 days of the statement; the provider must decide within 10 business days, or within 45 days with provisional credit. [CFPB: Regulation E 1005.11](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/11/)
- International transfers (remittances): you can cancel within 30 minutes after payment if the recipient has not picked up the money, and the provider must refund the full amount, including fees, within three business days. [CFPB: Regulation E 1005.34](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/34/)
- For a remittance error (for example the money was not made available by the promised date, or the wrong amount was paid or received), give notice within 180 days after the disclosed date of availability; the provider must investigate and decide within 90 days. [CFPB: Regulation E 1005.33](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/33/)
- Western Union says that if your transfer has not been picked up, sent or deposited, you get a full refund. [Western Union: report fraud](https://www.westernunion.com/us/en/fraudawareness/fraud-report-fraud.html)
- Zelle says certain impostor scams qualify for reimbursement, and unauthorized payments typically qualify. [Zelle: report a fraud or scam](https://www.zelle.com/support/report-scam)
- For a "payment sent by mistake" from a stranger, Venmo says to contact it as soon as possible so it can help reverse the payment, and Cash App says to refund the payment rather than send a new one. [Venmo: common scams](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/common-scams-on-venmo-vhel167) [Cash App: avoiding common scams](https://cash.app/help/us/en-us/6544-avoiding-common-scams)

## Traps

- Zelle payments cannot be reversed; you can cancel only if the recipient has not yet enrolled with Zelle. [Zelle FAQ](https://www.zelle.com/faq)
- Cash App's terms say a payment instruction error, such as sending to the wrong recipient, is not an unauthorized transaction, and warn that P2P scams may result in the loss of your funds with no recourse. [Cash App Terms of Service](https://cash.app/us/en/legal/tos)
- The Apple Cash terms say card-funded payments you are induced to make by an impostor or other fraud are not "unauthorized", and P2P transfers are non-refundable except as the terms provide or law requires. [Apple Cash terms](https://applecash.greendot.com/termsconditions/)
- PayPal's Friends and Family payments are not covered by Purchase Protection; a seller you do not know who asks for Friends and Family is a red flag. [PayPal: learn about scams](https://www.paypal.com/us/security/learn-about-scams)
- Wise says it cannot guarantee a refund for a scam payment; it may try to recall the money, and if you have not heard of a successful recall after 40 days, it was likely unsuccessful. [Wise: report fraud or a scam](https://wise.com/us/help/articles/2978048/how-do-i-report-fraud-or-a-scam)
- After a Western Union transfer is sent or deposited, Western Union may not be able to give you a refund. [Western Union: report fraud](https://www.westernunion.com/us/en/fraudawareness/fraud-report-fraud.html)
- Many user agreements require individual arbitration with a short opt-out window; Venmo's opt-out notice must be postmarked within 30 days after you first accept the agreement. [Venmo User Agreement](https://venmo.com/legal/us-user-agreement/)

## Scams to watch for

- Pay-yourself scam: a fake bank fraud alert text is followed by a call that seems to come from your bank; the caller gets your one-time passcode, enrolls their own account with your email or phone number, and the money you think you send to yourself goes to them. Zelle says your bank will never ask you to send money to yourself. [Zelle: pay-yourself scams](https://www.zelle.com/how-to-spot-a-pay-yourself-scam)
- Accidental payment: a stranger sends you money "by mistake" and asks you to send it back as a new payment; the scammer then disputes the original payment, so they are paid twice. [Cash App: avoiding common scams](https://cash.app/help/us/en-us/6544-avoiding-common-scams)
- Fake payment proof: when you sell to a stranger, a scammer sends screenshots of fake emails that make it look like they paid, or says the money arrives only after you ship. [Venmo: common scams](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/common-scams-on-venmo-vhel167)
- Fake invoices and money requests sent through PayPal itself ask you to call a phone number in the request; PayPal says not to call it. [PayPal: learn about scams](https://www.paypal.com/us/security/learn-about-scams)
- Code theft: a caller pretending to be Venmo asks you to read back the verification code sent to your phone; Venmo agents never ask for it. [Venmo: common scams](https://help.venmo.com/cs/articles/common-scams-on-venmo-vhel167)
- Cash flipping: someone promises to multiply your money if you first send a "clearance fee"; Cash App Support never asks you to send a payment or make a test transaction. [Cash App: recognize scams](https://cash.app/help/us/en-us/6482-recognize-scams-unauthorized-activity)
- Money transfer fraud usually starts with contact from a stranger: a check to deposit with part sent back, a relative in a crisis, or a prize that needs fees; MoneyGram's top tip is never to send money to someone you don't know. [MoneyGram: fraud and safety](https://www.moneygram.com/us/en/help-center/fraud-and-safety)

## By the numbers

- The CFPB received approximately 102,900 money transfer, money service and virtual currency complaints in 2025; companies closed 94% of those sent to them with an explanation, 2% with monetary relief and 2% with non-monetary relief. [CFPB 2025 Consumer Response Annual Report](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_2025-cr-annual-report_2026-03.pdf)
- In 2025 the monthly average of complaints about domestic (US) money transfers, the most-complained-about product type in this group, rose 1,317% from the prior two years' monthly average. [CFPB 2025 Consumer Response Annual Report](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_2025-cr-annual-report_2026-03.pdf)
- Bank payments had the highest aggregate fraud losses reported to the FTC in 2025, followed by cryptocurrency. [FTC testimony, March 25, 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/ftc-testimony-jec-hearing-on-the-rising-scam-economy.pdf)
